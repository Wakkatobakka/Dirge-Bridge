DIRGE BRIDGE v0.2.4 — VERIFICATION SUMMARY

Scope
- Bounded Weapon Aim repair only.
- Removed the failed v0.2.3 Action -> Weapon Aim pointer handoff.
- Weapon Aim / Field Camera is again an independent touch control.
- Baseline APK: exact v0.2.3 device-tested build.
- Only TwinInput and TwinInput$Contact are replaced in classes.dex.

Root cause found
- The compile-only CameraAssist stub used stale synthetic pad IDs:
  PAD_LEFT=23, PAD_RIGHT=24 (and the rest shifted similarly).
- The actual runtime CameraAssist uses PAD_LEFT=24, PAD_RIGHT=26, PAD_UP=25, PAD_DOWN=27.
- Because the old stub exposed those IDs as static-final constants, javac inlined the stale numbers into TwinInput. The right side of Weapon Aim could therefore be interpreted by the runtime as LEFT, while another direction could be ignored.
- v0.2.4 makes the compile-only PAD_* fields non-final runtime reads and aligns their documented values with the runtime.
- javap verification confirms TwinInput bytecode uses getstatic CameraAssist.PAD_LEFT and getstatic CameraAssist.PAD_RIGHT rather than inlined integer constants.

Control behavior
- Action remains independent.
- Weapon Aim / Field Camera remains horizontal-only.
- In first-person, the Weapon Aim strip feeds the same native left/right aim bridge already proven by the game.
- Vertical right-thumb drift remains ignored.
- Compact 4-way left D-pad, roll assist, field camera behavior, launcher shell, saves, renderer and audio are inherited unchanged from v0.2.3.
