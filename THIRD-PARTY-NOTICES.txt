G.726 / G.723-16 ADPCM algorithm
--------------------------------
The G72616.java decoder is a Java port of the classic Sun Microsystems
g72x/g723_16 reference algorithm. The reference implementation's notice states
that the material was developed by Sun Microsystems, Inc., is provided for
unrestricted use, and that users may copy or modify it without charge.

The port is included solely to decode the 2-bit ADPM audio streams already
present in a user's legally obtained Lost Episode game data. No game audio is
included in the redistributable bridge or preservation package.
